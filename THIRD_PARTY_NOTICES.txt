in frontend/src/data/pet-art.json. Portraits are local and require no network.

cdn.questlog.gg, visually checked against NCSOFT's official pet Guidebook.

frontend/src/data/pet-type-art.json. These icons are bundled for offline use.
The three spawn maps (Verteron, Altgard and Chaotic Lower Reshanta) bundle
original AION 2 client map textures at their native size. They are re-encoded as
lossy WebP (quality 95) for delivery; the lossless originals are kept, unbundled,
in artwork/originals/pet-map-art. Client build, texture paths, dimensions,
encoding settings and source, original and bundled SHA-256 hashes are in
frontend/src/data/pet-map-art.json. The maps are local and need no network.
Original class emblems are bundled from NCSOFT's public character website CDN,
and original skill icons from NCSOFT's public game-data CDN, unmodified.
Resource keys, source URLs, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
frontend/src/data/game-art.json; see docs/GAME_ARTWORK.md for provenance.
These game images are separate from the software and Lucide icon licenses.
The original Odyle energy icon is bundled unchanged from NCSOFT's game-data CDN.
Its resource key, source URL, dimensions and checksum are in
frontend/src/data/resource-art.json.
The original red Hidden Cube icon, P_Img_HiddenCube_01_V04, is also bundled
unchanged from NCSOFT's game-data CDN. Its resource key, source URL, native
package name, dimensions and checksum are in that same resource-art manifest.
See docs/GAME_ARTWORK.md for provenance. This artwork remains NCSOFT's and is
separate from the software and Lucide icon licenses.
